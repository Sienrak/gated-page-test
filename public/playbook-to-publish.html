Playbook instructions: Customer segmentation revenue maximizer

Global formatting rules: 1. Brand name: The company name "WRITER" must always be written in ALL-CAPS. 2. Header formatting: All headers must use sentence case (First word capitalized, all words after lowercase, except for proper nouns and acronyms).

1. Customer segmentation analysis

  • Query w-connector to extract customer data including lifetime value, credit score, customer segment, risk profile, and status
  • Calculate average engagement scores per customer by joining with marketing response data from w-connector
  • Segment customers into value tiers: High-Value (top 20%), Mid-Value (middle 30%), Low-Value (bottom 50%)
  • Analyze distribution of customers across segments by region, acquisition channel, and risk profile
  • Identify characteristics of top-performing customer segments (highest lifetime value + engagement)

2. Marketing performance by segment

  • Query w-connector to analyze campaign performance for each customer segment
  • Calculate response rates, conversion rates, and ROI by segment and channel
  • Identify which marketing channels work best for each customer segment
  • Find segments with high potential but low marketing engagement
  • Calculate average conversion value per segment

3. Revenue opportunity gap analysis

  • Compare actual revenue performance vs. potential for each segment
  • Identify high-value customers who are inactive or low-engagement (churn risk opportunities)
  • Find mid-value customers with low risk scores who could be upsold to premium products
  • Analyze segments with high engagement but low conversion (optimization opportunities)
  • Calculate total addressable revenue opportunity by segment and strategy

4. Revenue maximization strategy

  • Prioritize segments by revenue opportunity size and ease of execution
  • Develop specific targeting strategies for top 3 opportunity segments
  • Recommend optimal marketing channels and campaign types per segment
  • Create customer targeting criteria (credit score ranges, risk thresholds, engagement minimums)
  • Estimate expected revenue lift and required marketing investment per strategy

5. Actionable campaign recommendations

  • Generate specific campaign ideas with target audience definitions
  • Recommend budget allocation across segments and channels
  • Identify quick wins (high-value inactive customers, high-engagement non-converters)
  • Suggest A/B testing strategies to validate assumptions
  • Provide timeline and success metrics for each recommended campaign

6. Data visualization creation

  • Create interactive dashboard showing customer segment distribution with pie charts and bar graphs
  • Visualize lifetime value distribution across segments using box plots or histograms
  • Generate heat map showing channel performance by customer segment
  • Create waterfall chart illustrating revenue opportunity gaps by segment
  • Build scatter plot showing relationship between engagement score and lifetime value by segment
  • Visualize campaign ROI comparison across segments and channels using grouped bar charts
  • Create funnel visualization showing conversion rates from contact to conversion by segment

Completion

  • Deliver comprehensive revenue maximization report with executive summary and detailed findings
  • Include all SQL queries for easy implementation of targeting strategies
  • Provide interactive HTML dashboard with all visualizations embedded for easy sharing and presentation
  • Include data tables with segment definitions, opportunity sizing, and campaign recommendations
