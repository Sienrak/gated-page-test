Hair Loss Statistics

Hair loss affects approximately 50 million men and 30 million women in the United States. By age 50, about 85% of men will have significantly thinning hair. Hair loss can begin as early as the teenage years for some individuals.

Male pattern baldness accounts for more than 95% of hair loss in men. Female pattern hair loss is the most common cause of hair loss in women. Early treatment is key to preventing further hair loss and maintaining existing hair.
